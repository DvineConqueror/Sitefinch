# Sitefinch

A native Android camera and project album for field workers documenting jobs offline. Sitefinch preserves an unbranded JPEG for every saved photo. Workers use editable, reusable templates to add job context, then share a separate stamped or marked-up copy. The camera also records project clips with optional stamps and microphone audio. Projects can be shared as evidence ZIPs.

Every capture uses device time. Location can be precise, approximate, or unavailable. The share flow makes location disclosure explicit. The local digest helps users check whether a saved photo still matches the recorded hash.
