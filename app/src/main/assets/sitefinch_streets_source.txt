sitefinch_streets.bin is a gzip-compressed, adapted OpenStreetMap database, licensed under the Open Data Commons Open Database License v1.0 (ODbL): https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

Street and waterway centerlines cover a 0.15° × 0.10° area in Pangasinan, Philippines, including Dagupan and Mangaldan. The app source includes the adapted database in this folder. The maps embedded in photos and videos include visible attribution.

Source extracts from the OpenStreetMap map API, listed in packing order:

- https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/map?bbox=120.40,16.05,120.45,16.10
- https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/map?bbox=120.40,16.00,120.45,16.05
- https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/map?bbox=120.35,16.05,120.40,16.10
- https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/map?bbox=120.30,16.05,120.35,16.10
- https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/map?bbox=120.35,16.00,120.40,16.05
- https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/map?bbox=120.30,16.00,120.35,16.05

To refresh the database, download those bounding boxes in order and pass the saved XML extracts to scripts/build_street_map.py, following the path of the output asset. The script filters selected road classes and waterways, deduplicates ways by OpenStreetMap ID, and stores the selected names and geometry rounded to 0.00001°. Data is a snapshot and does not require a network connection at runtime.
