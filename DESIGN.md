# Work Photo Log design

## Field capture

Use the camera as the landing screen. The current project stays visible above a matte preview. Album and Location sit beside the shutter. Wide touch targets, immediate feedback, system bars, and Android Back keep the screen usable one-handed. A missing location or camera permission gets its own action and status.

Review puts the photo first, then gives the worker a place to add a note and preview a job stamp. Keep clean sharing as the primary option. Describe the ZIP and its optional GPS before opening the Android share sheet.

## Material and type

Warm paper surfaces support outdoor readability. Deep olive controls mark primary actions. Dark mode uses charcoal olive surfaces and a lighter action color. Keep icons and typography native in scale and weight.

| Role | Light | Dark |

Use platform motion settings and scalable text. Keep the Android camera and galleries visually distinct from the reference app.
